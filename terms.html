1. About the App

The App assists with travel planning. No account is required. It supports Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Korean display languages. These terms are published in Japanese and English.

2. Itineraries and accuracy

Itineraries, travel times, opening hours, crowd levels, and walking distances are all estimates; their accuracy is not guaranteed. Check official information from facilities and transport operators before departure. The App does not handle train or bus timetables or fares. Use the transit-directions control for each leg to open Google Maps or Apple Maps and check the details.

AI results for estimating places from photos and displaying place names in the local language are also estimates. AI results are not used to determine travel times, opening hours, or prices.

You are responsible for assessing traffic, weather, safety, and your health. Do not operate your phone while walking. The App has not verified allergy accommodations. Always check with the restaurant or shop before eating.

The App is a paid download ($1.99). Planning tickets are consumable in-app purchases available in packs for one or three trips. Apple processes payments. App purchasers receive one free trip, except when the App was obtained during a ¥0 (free) campaign.

5. Ticket validity and coverage

One ticket covers one trip and is valid for 48 hours. Within that validity period, you can replan repeatedly with the same ticket, even for a different travel date, if the starting point is within 30 km of the ticket's starting point. The usage limits below still apply.

6. Reservation, consumption, and automatic return

Opening a ticket reserves one trip from your balance. That trip is consumed when any paid feature (Google search, lists, photos, or AI) first succeeds. If none succeeds before the ticket expires, the reserved trip is automatically returned.
